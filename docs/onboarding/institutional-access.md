# Institutional Access Setup

This guide helps you access OHSU systems for your research internship.

## Required Accounts

Before Week 01 starts, verify you have:

1. **OHSU NetID** — Your institutional account username
2. **OHSU Email** — firstname.lastname@ohsu.edu
3. **OHSU Password** — Set in your account settings
4. **GitHub Account** — For code version control (you should already have this)

## Setting Up Multifactor Authentication (MFA)

OHSU requires multifactor authentication for security:

1. Go to https://my.ohsu.edu (OHSU's portal)
2. Log in with your NetID and password
3. Look for "Security" or "Multifactor Authentication" settings
4. Choose your preferred method:
   - SMS text message to your phone
   - Authenticator app (Authy, Google Authenticator)
   - Hardware security key
5. Set up and test it

**Keep backup codes safe** — Write them down and store them securely. They let you access your account if your phone is unavailable.

## Accessing OHSU Email

### On Web

1. Go to https://outlook.office.com
2. Log in with your OHSU email and password (+ MFA code)
3. You'll see your institutional email inbox

### On Your Computer

Most email clients work (Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird). Ask your IT support for setup instructions.

### Best Practice

Check your OHSU email at least once per week during your internship. Important institutional announcements go there.

## Accessing Microsoft Teams

3. Look for "Conrad Lab" or similar team/channel
4. Eisa or Don will send you an invitation link to join

## Troubleshooting Access Issues

### "Login failed" or "Invalid credentials"

- Double-check your username (NetID, not email)
- Verify caps lock is off (passwords are case-sensitive)
- Reset password if you're unsure: https://my.ohsu.edu

### MFA code not working

- Time on your phone might be wrong (sync time setting)
- Backup codes can be used instead (request new ones from IT)
- Contact OHSU IT Support: (503) 494-4357

### Can't see Teams or email

- Might need to wait 24 hours after account creation
- Try logging out and back in
- Clear browser cache
- Contact OHSU IT Support if still blocked

### Email or Teams working on web but not in app

- Might need to add account to email client
- Sometimes requires special password (app password)
- Contact your mentors or IT support for email client setup

## Security Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Keep your password confidential
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable multifactor authentication
- Log out when done (especially on shared computers)
- Report suspicious activity to IT

❌ **Don't:**
- Share your NetID or password
- Write passwords on sticky notes
- Use OHSU accounts for personal projects
- Access OHSU systems from unsecured networks (use VPN)
